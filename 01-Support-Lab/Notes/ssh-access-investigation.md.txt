# SSH Access Investigation

## Goal
To observe SSH authentication logs and determine if the password attempts showed evidence of an attack

## Setup
MacBook Air with 16GB ram, Ubuntu run on VirtualBox VM, and forwarding rule for MAC 127.0.0.1:2222 on Ubuntu VM port 22

## Evidence
Successful logins showed "Accepted password" and deliberately failed logins showed "Failed password"

checked the logs from the last 10 minutes using: sudo journalctl -u ssh.service --since "10 minutes ago" --no-pager

## Conclusion
Intentionally entered an incorrect password attempt and observed the corresponding failure. The log sequence alone neither proves nor rules out an attack

## Lesson Learned
--since differs from --vacuum-time in that --since "10 minutes ago" only displays logs from the last 10 minutes and --vacuum-time=10 removes eligible archived journal files older than 10 seconds

Not every failed password attempt means an attack

I did not verify whether the vacuum command deleted any files
